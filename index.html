Privacy Policy

Updated: September 2, 2026

1. General Provisions

The Converter app processes all files entirely on the user's device.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

The app does not collect or share with third parties: file contents and names, photos, videos, audio recordings, documents, geolocation and contact information, device identifiers for advertising purposes, user behavior data, or analytics. The app works offline and makes no network requests.

3. Your Files

Audio, video, photos, documents and other files opened in the app are used solely to perform conversion, editing, and saving of results. Files never leave the device: all operations are performed locally using built-ins iOS capabilities.

4. Access to Files and Photos

The app requests access to Files and Photos solely to import materials explicitly selected by the user for processing. No other device content is accessed.

5. Temporary Files

During processing, the app may create temporary copies of files in local storage. These copies are automatically deleted after the corresponding operation completes or is cancelled.

6. Operation History

Information about completed operations is stored only on the user's device and can be deleted at any time in the app settings.

7. Third-Party Components

The app may use open-source libraries for local file processing. All such components run solely on the user's device and do not transmit, store, or process any data off-device.

8. Tracking and Advertising

The app does not track users and does not use analytics or advertising technologies.

9. Children

The app is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from minors.

10. Security

Access to the device and the data stored on it is protected by standard iOS operating system mechanisms.

11. Policy Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The new version takes effect from the moment it is published in the app. The date of the latest revision is indicated at the beginning of this document.
